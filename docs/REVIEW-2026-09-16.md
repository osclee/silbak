# Silbak — Launch Readiness Review

Date: 2026-09-16 · Reviewer: Claude (Opus 5) · Owner: Oz
Engine version reviewed: `ENGINE_VERSION = 3` · Web at commit `35496fc` (master)
Trigger: *"Assess the readiness of Silbak for live usage. I am thinking about adding it to a real domain and sharing."*

> **Scope note.** This is a narrower review than [`REVIEW-2026-09-13.md`](REVIEW-2026-09-13.md): that one asked "is the game good?" and answered across gameplay, tuning and code health. This one asks one question — **what breaks, embarrasses or leaks the moment this is a public URL people are invited to?** Where the two disagree, this document is the later reading. Part A is the assessment; Part B is the plan, with Phase 1 already shipped in the same change that added this file.

---

## Part A — Assessment

### A.1 Method

- Read all of `packages/engine/src` and `apps/web/src`, plus CI, `staticwebapp.config.json` and `index.html`.
- `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`, `pnpm build`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test` from a clean clone — all green (17 tests, 6 files).
- Played the daily to a win in a real Chromium at iPhone 12 (390×664) and desktop (1280×900), driving the actual tap-swap path; measured layout with `getBoundingClientRect` rather than by eye.
- Measured the deployed asset payload from `apps/web/dist` after a real build.
- Audited the shipped state of every finding in `REVIEW-2026-09-13.md` Part B rather than trusting the plan.
- Verified epoch/timezone arithmetic directly against the engine (`puzzleNumber` for dates around `EPOCH`).

### A.2 Verdict

**The game is launch-grade. The shell around it was not.**

Phases 1, 2, 3 and most of 4 of the September 13 plan are genuinely shipped, and the parts of the app a player touches during a game are in good shape. What was missing was almost entirely *the things that only matter once strangers arrive*: a phone layout, a link in the share text, a preview card, and a couple of things that would have been mildly embarrassing on a domain carrying the author's real name.

Working, verified by play rather than by reading:

- **The engine.** Pure, deterministic, zero runtime dependencies, 17 passing tests over a 365-day golden snapshot. The keystone is sound and nothing in this review touches generation.
- **The loop.** Arrange → submit → non-terminal verdict → win → streak → stats → persistence all correct on first play, with **zero console errors** at every viewport tested.
- **Spoiler discipline.** The solution is stripped at the `puzzle.ts` boundary and held in a module closure, never in store state. `/archive/<today>` and `/archive/99999999` both redirect rather than leak or crash. `/dev/apes` is confirmed absent from the production bundle (0 matches in `dist`).
- **Persistence.** Versioned envelope, fails safe to a clean wipe, migrates v1, caps `played` at 60 entries.
- **Deploy.** CI gates the Azure Static Web Apps deploy on typecheck + tests. CSP, `nosniff` and `Referrer-Policy` were already set, and the app makes no external requests, so the policy is honest rather than aspirational.

### A.3 Findings

Severity: **L0** = would damage the launch · **L1** = fix before sharing widely · **L2** = decide deliberately.

#### L0

**1. The board was not playable on a phone.** Measured at 390×664 before the change: first rung at **y=471**, Submit at **y=1052** on a 664px viewport, page 2.45 screens, rung heights ragged between **82 and 95px**. A game whose entire mechanic is comparing six things and committing to an order required scrolling between the six things and the button. Root cause was horizontal, not vertical: the `12ch` rank column (96px, 25% of the viewport, to render "SILVERBACK") squeezed the trait line into three and four wraps, and the ragged heights followed from that.

Secondary, same area: `.hintRow` used `justify-content: space-between` with **no gap**, so at 390px its two strings each wrapped to two lines and interleaved, rendering as `6 GUESSES LEFT · PAR TAP OR DRAG AN APE TO MOVE / 4 IT` — one garbled sentence made of two sensible ones.

**2. The share text had no link in it.** `shareGrid` emitted the header and the emoji grid and stopped (Phase 5.3 of the previous plan, never shipped). A result pasted into a group chat — which is the entire growth mechanism of a daily game — gave a reader no way to reach the game. Compounding it, `index.html` carried **no OG or Twitter metadata at all**, so pasting the domain itself unfurled as a bare link.

**3. Difficulty has never been validated on the real UI, and the last measurement overshot.** `DESIGN.md` §9.6 records the v3 gate honestly: the target was avg 3.0–3.5 guesses, ≥30% needing 4+, losses under ~5%. The blind playtest returned **avg 4.05**, **66% needing 4+**, **5.3% losses**, from **38 of a planned 75 puzzles** before the run was stopped, with no qualitative notes surviving. Monday — the gentlest weekday, par 3 — had three of five agents take five guesses. The recorded decision was to ship and *"sanity-check with real play on the ledger UI before launch"*. **That sanity-check has not happened.** The agents also played through the CLI with no ledger, which is precisely the aid count-only feedback was designed to lean on, so the figure may be pessimistic — but nobody knows by how much.

This is the one L0 finding this change does **not** fix, because it cannot be fixed by code. See Phase 2.

#### L1

**4. `EPOCH` was launch day itself,** with two consequences. The archive was **empty on day one** — a visitor who liked the game had exactly one puzzle and nothing else to try, and `Archive.tsx` renders an empty list with no empty-state copy. And because `todayKey()` is deliberately local (matching Wordle's local-midnight rollover) while `puzzleNumber` counts from a UTC epoch, **players west of UTC saw "Troop #0"** — verified: `2026-09-15 → #0`, `2026-09-14 → #-1`.

**5. `index.html` was stale.** The meta description said "Rank **five** apes" — wrong since v0.3 — and `theme-color` was `#0D1913`, the dark palette retired in v0.7, which paints mobile browser chrome near-black above a cream page.

**6. 591KB of a 940KB deploy was fonts.** 50 files: Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese and latin-ext subsets, pulled by unqualified `@fontsource/<family>/400.css` imports, for a game whose entire text is English. Flagged as Phase 7.1 on September 13 and not shipped. It is the largest single performance win available and it costs five lines.

**7. "Karisoke" is a real institution.** The eyebrow on every board read "Karisoke observation log". Karisoke is the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund's research centre. Harmless flavour on a private prototype; on a public domain with the author's real name in the footer it reads as a claim of affiliation nobody granted.

**8. The footer printed a personal email in plain text,** which is harvested within days of a domain being indexed.

**9. Dead deploy config.** `staticwebapp.config.json` routed `/api/profile` with `allowedRoles: ["authenticated"]` and declared `platform.apiRuntime: node:20` against an API that does not exist, and excluded a `*.webmanifest` that is never produced.

#### L2 — decide, don't default

- **No analytics and no error reporting.** There will be no way to know whether anyone plays, where they drop, or whether something broke for everyone. Deliberately left out of this change: it is a privacy and dependency decision, not a bug.
- **No LICENSE, and the repository is public** — so it is "all rights reserved" by default. Fine if intended; it should be intended rather than inherited.
- **No countdown** ("Next troop in hh:mm") on the result card — Phase 4.5 of the previous plan, still the one missing retention beat.
- **No `robots.txt` or web manifest.**
- **Residual header height on mobile.** After Phase 1 the first rung sits at y=414 (from 471). The remaining bulk is the field-notes panel, which is the puzzle itself and cannot be cut. The stated goal — ladder and Submit visible together — is met; "first rung above y≈260", the target the September 13 plan proposed, is arithmetically unreachable while the notes sit above the ladder, and should be treated as retired rather than outstanding.

## Part B — Plan

### Phase 1 — The launch pass ✅ *shipped 2026-09-16, with this document*

Everything in this phase is web-and-config only; generation, feedback semantics and the clue pool are untouched, and `ENGINE_VERSION` stays at 3.

1. **Mobile board** (L0 #1). Below 480px: the rank column collapses `12ch → 2.5ch` by swapping the word badge for the numeral (two spans in `Rung.tsx`, one always `display:none`, both `aria-hidden` — `aria-label` carries the full semantics at every width); the portrait steps `56px → 44px`, its §6-stated floor; vertical padding drops to `--space-2`; the wordmark drops to 2rem and the rules paragraph to its two essential sentences. Trait separators became elements so their width is a style (0.4em → 0.22em), which keeps the longest trait string in the game on one line at 375px **without truncating** — every word there is a clue. `.hintRow` gained a gap at all widths and stacks below 480px.
   - **Result, measured:** rung heights uniform at **62px** (from 82–95); ladder-plus-Submit **490px**, fitting one viewport at 390×664, 375×812 and desktop; first rung y=471 → **414**; no horizontal overflow; no console errors.
2. **Share URL and link preview** (L0 #2). `SITE_URL` in `apps/web/src/lib/share.ts` — web-side, not in the engine, which stays deployment-agnostic — appended as a trailing line. Full OG/Twitter block in `index.html` with absolute URLs, plus `canonical`. `og.png` is rendered 1200×630 by a new `apps/web/scripts/render-og.mjs` from the real wordmark, the real tokens and the real `favicon.svg`, with the shipped font files embedded as data URIs so it cannot silently rasterise in a fallback face. Wired into `pnpm --filter @silbak/web icons`.
3. **`EPOCH` back fourteen days** to `2026-09-02` (L1 #4). Puzzles are keyed by `dateKey`, so this renumbers without changing content — verified: `2026-09-16` still yields solution `[1,5,0,2,4,3]`, space 174, identical troop, now as #15. Golden snapshot regenerated deliberately. The sub-epoch "Troop #0" case is removed by construction rather than clamped.
4. **Metadata, fonts, brand, footer, config** (L1 #5–9). Description corrected to six apes; `theme-color` → `--paper`. Font imports → `latin-` entry points: **591KB → 186KB, 50 files → 10**, and the CSS bundle fell 33.4KB → 22.4KB with it. Eyebrow → "Mikeno observation log" (a Virunga peak — the register of the ape names, and not an institution). Footer byline links to the author instead of printing an address. Dead `/api/*` route and `apiRuntime` removed; `frame-ancestors 'none'`, `base-uri` and `form-action` added to the CSP.

**Verification run:** `pnpm typecheck && pnpm test` green (17/17); full win play-through at iPhone 12 including a non-terminal guess, with the share text confirmed to carry the URL and the archive confirmed to list 14 days.

### Phase 2 — Difficulty sanity-check on the real UI *(before sharing widely)*

The open L0. Not code, and not delegable to the CLI harness, because the thing being tested is whether the ledger closes the gap between the model's careful human (2.9) and the agents' trait-blind bound (4.05).

1. Play a full week in the browser, on the ledger, without looking at solutions — Monday and Saturday especially. `/archive` now has fourteen backdated days, which is exactly the sample.
2. Record guesses-to-solve per weekday against `PAR` (Mon 3 · Tue–Thu 4 · Fri 4 · Sat 5 · Sun 3).
3. If Monday still runs to five guesses, `PAR` is wrong before `BANDS` is — retune par first, since it is display-only and changes no puzzle. Only if the curve itself is wrong does this become a `BANDS` sweep, and that is a `CLAUDE.md`-governed engine change with a version bump.
4. Re-run the `playtest-puzzles` skill against fresh numbers if the manual week is ambiguous — but with the ledger in the loop this time, or the result will reproduce the same bias.

### Phase 3 — Know whether it worked *(L2, decide first)*

Analytics and error reporting. Privacy-respecting and self-hosted or nothing; the CSP's `connect-src 'self'` is currently a true statement and worth keeping true. Without this, the Phase 2 retune has no data to succeed it and `PAR`'s "provisional until a month of real `played` entries exists" (§5) can never resolve.

### Phase 4 — Remaining polish *(L2)*

`LICENSE` decision · result-card countdown to local midnight · `robots.txt` and web manifest · archive empty state (now unreachable at fourteen days, but cheap insurance).
